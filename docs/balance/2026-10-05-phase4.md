# Balance after Phase 4 — the mastery board rebuilt (2026-10-05)

Phase 4 rebuilt the mastery board (version 2). Every node now speaks gear's stat language or a board-only stat (lifesteal, status power, Rift capacity), so nothing on it is read by nobody and every branch's name says what it gives. Each branch runs keystone (4 levels) → five fillers → **Tincture** (2, a rule that changes how you play) → six fillers → **Opus** (4, its capstone). The four Status keystones are the humours (Choler, Phlegm, Sanguine, Melancholy) and let any element leave that status. The second-ability switch moved to its own node, **the Rebis**, at the centre: any build reaches it, it locks nothing, and it's hidden for an element with one ability. Space is now Range vs Area. `SphereGrid.cs` and the old `MasteryStat` enum are gone. Compare with `2026-10-05-phase3.md`.

By the user's call the balance check waits until after Phase 5; the numbers below record where the board leaves things.

The regression check still passes (`Assay.Check`: Reference and BuiltThree, worst 2e-7) and `ElementNumbers.Drift()` reports `none`.

## Every rule on the board

|---|---|---|
| Strikes | Tincture **Vitriol** | Every hit on the same enemy as your last bites 2% deeper, up to 10%. A new target starts over. |
| Strikes | Opus **Cohobation** | Every basic that lands steeps your next weapon art: +8% each, up to +40%. The weapon art spends it. |
| Weapon Arts | Tincture **Cinnabar** | A PERFECT weapon art always crits. |
| Weapon Arts | Opus **Multiplication** | Your chain is one basic shorter (never fewer than one): weapon arts come sooner. |
| Element | Tincture **Alkahest** | A release hands back 20% of your meter. |
| Element | Opus **Exaltation** | For 5 seconds after a release, +25% damage. |
| Burn | keystone **Choler** | Your weapon arts set what they hit burning: 10% of the hit each second for 3 seconds. Any element. |
| Burn | Tincture **Saltpetre** | Burning enemies take 10% more from your hits. |
| Burn | Opus **Cineration** | A burning enemy that dies bursts, setting everything within 2.5 units burning with the burn it carried. |
| Soak | keystone **Phlegm** | Your weapon arts soak what they hit for 3 seconds: slowed, and taking 12% more damage. Any element. |
| Soak | Tincture **Aqua Fortis** | Soaking an enemy also soaks the nearest other enemy within 3 units. |
| Soak | Opus **Solution** | Soaked enemies deal 25% less damage to you. |
| Bleed | keystone **Sanguine** | Your crits open a bleed: 15% of the hit each second for 4 seconds. Any element. |

| Bleed | Opus **Mortification** | Your crits on a bleeding enemy deal 30% more. |
| Stagger | keystone **Melancholy** | Your weapon arts stagger what they hit for 2 seconds: slowed, more with each stagger. Any element. |
| Stagger | Tincture **Antimony** | Staggered enemies attack 30% slower. |
| Stagger | Opus **Congelation** | An enemy staggered a third time in a row congeals: rooted for 1.5 seconds and taking 20% more damage. |
| Armour | Tincture **Alum** | While at full health, you take 30% less damage. |
| Armour | Opus **Fixation** | No single hit can take more than 25% of your max health. |
| Evasion | Tincture **Sal Ammoniac** | Your defensive ability's protection and parry window last 50% longer. |
| Evasion | Opus **Volatilization** | A parry refunds your defensive ability's cooldown. |
| Sustain | Tincture **Aqua Vitae** | Every kill heals 2% of your max health. |
| Sustain | Opus **Cibation** | Lifesteal held back by the healing limit becomes a shield, up to 20% of your max health, fading over 5 seconds. |
| Speed | Tincture **Realgar** | After a weapon art lands, your next 2 basics swing 20% faster. |
| Speed | Opus **Circulation** | Hits landed within 1 second of each other build flow: +3% attack speed each, up to +30%. A second without a hit loses it. |
| Weight | Tincture **Tartar** | Standing still, +12% damage. |
| Weight | Opus **Precipitation** | Your weapon arts land with a shockwave: 40% of the hit to every other enemy within 2 units of the target. |
| Range | Tincture **Aqua Regia** | Hits on an enemy beyond 66.7% of your reach slow it by 30% for 1.5 seconds. |
| Range | Opus **Inceration** | Enemies you have slowed take 15% more from your hits. |
| Area | Tincture **Borax** | Your releases and area weapon arts draw enemies 1.5 units toward their centre. |
| Area | Opus **Fermentation** | Your releases and area weapon arts salt the ground for 3 seconds: enemies on it are slowed by 30% and take 10% of your hit each second. |
| Rift | keystone **Cucurbit**, Opus **Aludel** | +1 Rift capacity each (five fillers between carry only their principle point) |
| centre | **The Rebis** | Choose which ability your release fires. One is active at a time, on the same button - switch here, free. Cost 1, no principle, hidden for an element with one ability. |

Status POWER (a status branch's primary, +45% at a full branch) also strengthens the element's own: Fire's Ignite burn and Erupt pools' burn, Water's soak, Earth's quake stagger.

**Not priced by the model** (single target, GOOD presses, no dodging): Cinnabar (needs PERFECT), Aqua Fortis, Cineration, Precipitation, Borax, Fermentation (crowds), Sal Ammoniac, Volatilization (dodging/parrying), Aqua Regia (control; it feeds Inceration's uptime). Everything else has a term in `Balance/AssayBoard.cs`.

## The wave budget

