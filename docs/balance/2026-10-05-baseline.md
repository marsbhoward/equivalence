# Balance baseline — 2026-10-05 (before the rebalance)

Phase 0 of the rebalance: today's game measured by the Assay (`Assets/Scripts/Balance/`), so every later phase has something to compare against. Nothing in the game reads the Assay yet.

Every guess the model makes is in `Assay.Assume` (how often a player stands still, Fire's average heat, Air's average momentum, a release every 15 s of fighting, ...); entries whose worth is a judgement call are in `Assay.Judged`. Regenerate any table with the eval named above it.

Kits: **Max** = full board at the 50 cap, Gold 3★ weapon + the build's three best slots at Gold 3★, every other slot filled at Gold 1★, all rolled toward the build. **Ceiling** = every slot Gold 3★. DPS is in multiples of the unbuffed, element-less player on floor 1 at the real swing cadence (39.6). Survival is in ordinary Chaser hits to die on that floor.

## Refusal caps and deal cadence on today's catalogue
Refusing is free today; the sensible player skips most offers. With a deal every floor, any run-wide cap forces enough picks (69+) to drain the whole pool - 70 boon stacks, 69 cost stacks - so every capped run ends holding everything, like random picks.
