# Balance after Phase 2 — elements, releases, spires, hazards (2026-10-05)

Phase 2 moved every element number into `Tuning.Elements`, re-expressed the elements as head starts in stat points (joined to the character's raw points before the curve) and their releases in hit units × Elemental Power × Area, rebuilt Fire's ladder (Fuel, Stoke, Ignite; burn no longer passive), calibrated all four for parity with `Assay.CalibrateElements`, put the spire boons on the run layer under their planetary-metal names, scaled pit damage by depth, matched tornado frequency to pit frequency, and made water pits soak the player. Compare with `2026-10-05-phase1.md`.

The regression check still passes (`Assay.Check`: Reference and BuiltThree replay PlayerPower on both ledgers, worst 2e-7), and `ElementNumbers.Drift()` reports `none`.

## What changed in the game

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Elements | All numbers in `Tuning.Elements`; head starts are stat points (`DamagePoints`, `AttackSpeedPoints`, `MoveSpeedPoints`, `CritDamagePoints`) bent with the character's own; crit, lifesteal and DR join the shared pools |
| Releases | Counted in hit units (`PlayerController.HitUnit`) × Elemental Power (`ReleaseScale`) × Area on radii |
| Fire | Rung 1 Fuel (a swing on a burning enemy banks 2 stacks), rung 3 Stoke (heat fades half as fast); burn moved out of the passive into Erupt's pools and the new Ignite window (8 s, every hit burns 35 % of itself) |
| Water | Tier-3 burst now lands before its own soak (it was 35 % bigger than priced) |
| Spires | Copper/Lead/Iron/Quicksilver/Silver/Gold/Tin; floor boons are run-layer points through the Vessel (Haste and Swiftness were multipliers compounding with the ledger) |
| Pits | Fire and spike damage × `FloorDifficulty.Damage` (tornadoes and spire lines already were) |
| Tornadoes | Storm chance = chance of any one pit kind (`HazardBuilder.PitKindChance`): 17.5 % of floors below 25, ~37 % from 25 |
| Water pits | Soak the player: +25 % damage taken from everything while wading and 3 s after, outside the mitigation floor; a leap clears it, a dash doesn't |

## Verified in play (immune player, saves backed up and restored)

- **Fire:** one stack per swing, not per body; Fuel 1 → 3 stacks on a burning target; haste +28 points from 2 stacks; Stoke measured 1.25 s of fade over 2.49 s; Erupt 7 pools at 7.7 HU/s × Elemental Power; Ignite 8 s, hit 20 → burn 8.68/s (= 20 × 0.35 × 1.24), no release inside the window.
- **Air:** full momentum +60 AS points (bent to 64.9 with the character's own 9), Gust spends momentum, +35 damage points × Elemental Power, +50 move, +1.3 range, 15 % lifesteal into the pool; Squall 5 vortices at 4.6 / 0.9 HU/s for 3.2 s.
- **Water:** tier-3 burst on one target 49.6 (4 HU × 1.24, not ×1.35), target soaked after, 9 s surge at +37.5 AS points.
- **Earth:** first shock 103.79 = 6.2 HU × 1.24; full quake with three aftershocks 250.13, exactly the modelled 2.41 shocks.
- **Soak:** damage taken 1.249 → 1.562 (×1.25) in water, held 3 s after leaving, then cleared.
- **Spires (edit mode):** at mastery 50 Iron +30 %, Quicksilver +25 %, Silver +18.8 % (past the run knee of 15), Gold +15 % crit, Tin −25 %; at mastery 0 Iron +24.8 %, Quicksilver +16.6 %, Silver +9.1 %.
