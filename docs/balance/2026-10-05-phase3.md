# Balance after Phase 3 — gear and weapon tables (2026-10-05)

Phase 3 rebuilt the gear tables. **One pool per slot**: every stat in a slot's pool can roll as its primary or as a sub-stat (the user's call), and all 25 rollable stats, Accuracy included, sit on the slots whose theme they fit. **Magnitudes re-based**: knee stats sized so one fully targeted Gold 3★ piece fills half the knee; the crit family (Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Accuracy) sized so a roll is worth a roll of Damage on the Max player; Resilience at Max HP's scale, Graze/Brace at twice it. **Weapon primary ×1.5** (`Tuning.GearRoll.WeaponPrimaryMul`). **Armour Max HP floor** cut from 3/6 to 1.25/2.5 (Max: 74 → ~31 points, half the Max HP knee). **Minted records migrate**: `MintedGearRecord.StatsVersion` + `GearForge.Migrate` keep every stored sub-stat roll at the same place in its new range. Compare with `2026-10-05-phase2.md`.

By the user's call the balance check waits until after Phase 5, so this phase does not chase the scorecard; the numbers below are the record of where the new tables leave it.

The regression check still passes (`Assay.Check`: Reference and BuiltThree, worst 2e-7) and `ElementNumbers.Drift()` reports `none`.
