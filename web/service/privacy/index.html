Privacy

What the game keeps about you, and why. Last updated 3 November 2026.

What we keep

  • Your saves - your characters, gear and progress. As a guest they are tied to a key kept in this browser; signed in, to your wallet's address.
  • Your wallet address, if you sign in with one - to load your saves and show the NFTs you hold. Signing in asks your wallet to sign a message, never a transaction.
  • Anonymous play data - how runs go: floors reached, how a run ended, bosses, deals taken, timing results, the kind of device and how smoothly the game ran, and any errors. It is marked with a code made from your character, not your name, wallet or address.

What we don't

  • We don't keep your IP address. Our host's server logs may record it for a short time.
  • No tracking cookies and no ads. The browser keeps your sign-in only so you stay signed in.
  • Nothing is sold or shared.

Why

To keep your progress, and to see where the game needs work.

How long

Everything from the showcase is deleted when the full beta opens. The data is stored on servers run by Railway.
