The Short Descent

Fifty floors, one hour

A full run fits in an evening. Waves of creatures fill the floors between the bosses. A mini-boss waits every ten floors, and an elemental avatar waits at 25 and at 50. The run clock is an hourglass. It lies on its side while you choose between floors and stands up again when the next floor's first enemy appears.

Difficulty by depth

Each mini-boss you beat makes the creatures smarter. First they lead their shots. Later they flank, punish whiffed attacks and take more turns at once. They never get faster tells.

Floors that change

Puzzle floors, capture spires that pay a boon, shaped rooms, fire and spike pits, and tornadoes. Rooms are open at the edges and drop away into the sky.

Places the tower bled into

Stretches of the climb turn into the Drowned Forest, the Sky Ruin or the Salt Flats. Near the top, the Nigredo covers everything in cinders and red light.

Combat

Deliberate, readable, punishing

Every attack is a commitment. Every enemy has a tell. A pack shares a limited number of attack turns, so a crowd stays readable. Careless play gets you hurt at every depth. Careful play is how you survive.

Two basics, then the art

Each chain is two basic attacks that lead into a weapon art. Arts are rolled during the run and swapped at the reward rings. Each one pulls, pushes, repositions or defends.

The timing bar

A weapon art's finisher rides a timing bar. A perfect press adds damage and builds a streak that raises your critical chance. A late press costs you.

Clashes

When two attacks meet, they cancel. Read the swing, meet it, and the blow never lands.

The phial

You get a few drinks per run, and each boss you beat refills one. Drinking roots you in place, and a hit spills the charge.

Weapon classes

Three ways to fight

You pick a class when the run starts and keep it to the end. Each class has its own basic attacks, its own arts and its own grip.

Melee

Greatsword

Heavy, two-handed and committed. It has the highest damage per swing, and its arts carve through crowds or punish a single target.

Hybrid

Twin discs

Heavy cuts up close, ricocheting throws at range, and it switches between them on its own. The throws are a dance. The first throw builds momentum with a spin, a hop or a leap, and the off hand spends it.

Pure range

Bow

No melee at any distance. The bow's job is making space: kneeling shots, recoil shots, twin nocks, and arts that kite the pack away from you.

The four elements

An element is a way to play

Every element fills a seal in its own way. When the seal is full, you spend it on a release. Each element gives a head start toward the same caps, so no element is the right one.

Fire

Heat builds when you land a hit, take one or parry. It fades if you stop fighting. Burning enemies feed it faster.

Water

The tide rises with every swing that lands, and a miss drains it. Precise play keeps it full. Its releases are its identity.

Earth

Slow swings and a stubborn body. The seal shakes, cracks and lifts stones as the tremor builds.

Air

The streak is accuracy. Light hits, a high critical chance, and blinks that put you where the pack isn't.

The Exchange

Nothing is given

After every floor you're offered deals, each one weighed on a bronze scale. Every boon comes paired with a cost, and the beam tips toward whichever side is heavier. Refuse a deal and it's gone. Accept it and you carry both for the rest of the run.

BoonBehaviours, not numbers

Boons change how your weapon and element behave: new forms for your arts, new statuses, new ways to set them off.

CostPaid every floor

Costs stack up to three deep. Armour wears faster, heals weaken, or the floors push back harder.

Rifts

Extract, or push on

A Rift opens around every boss. Through the tear you can see the room where your run began. Anything you've carried this far is at risk until you get it home.

Carried

Everything in the bag. If you die or leave without reaching another Rift, it's lost.

Secured

Only a few items fit through a Rift, and you choose them one by one. A sure thing now, or the diamond still in the bag?

Never lost

Experience and mastery stay with you whatever happens. Death costs you loot, never progress.

Mini-bosses · floors 10, 20, 30, 40

Fights with their own rules

The pool of mini-bosses is bigger than the four boss floors, so every run meets a different set. Each fight is a puzzle you learn by playing it.

VOICE

The faceless cantor

It sings a phrase and teleports between slices of the floor. You answer by stepping the same phrase back, one beat at a time.

GAZE

The serpent judge

Hide behind pillars from a gaze that turns you to stone. She breaks the pillars, and the rubble rebuilds.

GROVE

The beetle knight

A knight in an overgrown grove who blooms a lotus to turn the fight.

TIDE

The bronze serpent

A water-and-bronze bullet hell. You break off a limb in each phase.

WING

The fallen duellist

A plumed dark knight with a steel wing and a dying-star blade. A one-on-one duel at your own pace.

DICE

The gambler

Four games of skill with shuffled, glinting tells. When she's cornered, she strips off her armour and goes all in.

Floor25

You, in the past

A human avatar, geared and armed the way you are. It has an element and a weapon of its own and is still learning them.

Floor50

You, in the future

The final boss has mastered its element. Its phases vary more, and it may be awakened. What it says at the end depends on the element you carry.

Gear, the Forge and the hub

Twelve slots, one silhouette

Armour and weapons are drawn texel by texel and worn on a rig that turns in four directions. At the Forge you can add stars, re-roll sub-stats, combine pieces and dye cloth. Between runs you return to the hub. You can theme it, hang your weapons in the armoury, and pose for the photo booth.

Mastery

A board with a sigil at its centre and four forked paths. It unlocks your releases and shapes your build. Reincarnate to take a new route.

Sets that forge

Some Diamond pieces come in sets. Three swords forge into one, and four bird bows forge into a phoenix.

Yours to keep

Rare gear can be minted as a token on Cardano, signed with your character's name. Bring your own NFTs into the hub.
